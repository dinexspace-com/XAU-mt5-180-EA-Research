- Total Net Profit is negative.
- Profit Factor is below 1.0.

- Maximum balance and equity drawdown exceed 77%.

- Recovery Factor is negative.
- Sharpe Ratio is negative.
- The balance curve shows a persistent downward trend over the test period.

This result is intentionally retained as research evidence. Failed backtests are part of the research process and should not be removed or hidden.

## Backtest Policy

Backtest results represent historical simulations and do not guarantee future trading performance.

Results should not be classified as successful based only on profitability. Evaluation should also consider:

- Net Profit
- Profit Factor
- Maximum Drawdown
- Expected Payoff
- Recovery Factor
- Sharpe Ratio
- Winning and losing trade distribution
- Number of trades
- Balance curve stability
- Performance consistency across different market periods
- Robustness under different parameters and market conditions

Failed experiments are preserved because they provide useful evidence for strategy research, debugging, optimization, and future comparison.

The current EA-051 result is considered a **baseline failed experiment** and should be used as a reference point for subsequent research or strategy modifications.
